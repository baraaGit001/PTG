# 2026-09-04 (8)

## Did
- Replaced the phone bottom tab bar with an off-canvas sidebar drawer.
  `MobileBottomNav` is gone from
  [app-shell.tsx](../../packages/ui/src/composite/app-shell.tsx); in its place
  are `MobileSidebar` (Radix dialog, slides in from the inline-start edge) and
  `MobileNavTrigger` (the hamburger).
- Factored the nav body out into a shared `SidebarNav`, so desktop and mobile
  render the same tree from the same code. The mobile drawer now shows the
  `ME_NAV` sections too — the bottom bar could only ever show the five
  `PRIMARY_NAV` items.
- `AppShell` swapped its `bottomNav` prop for `mobileSidebar`, and `<main>`
  lost the bottom-bar padding reservation (now just `1.5rem + safe-area`).
- [root-layout.tsx](../../apps/web/src/layouts/root-layout.tsx) holds the open
  state and puts the hamburger left of the brand in `TopHeader`, mobile only.
- Added `common.menu` to all five locales (en/ar/es/ja/zh-CN) for the trigger
  and drawer title.
- Dropped the now-unused `bottom-nav` spacing token from
  [tailwind-preset.cjs](../../packages/config/tailwind-preset.cjs) and fixed
  the comments that still named `MobileBottomNav`.

## Decided
- Drawer, not a permanently visible mini-sidebar: the phone viewport is too
  narrow to give up horizontal space, and a drawer is the only shape that fits
  primary + "Me" nav without truncating either. Not ADR-worthy — it's a
  UI-shape call, visible in the code.
- Drawer opens from the inline-start edge (`start-0`, `border-e`, `ltr:`/`rtl:`
  slide variants) so Arabic gets it on the right without a second component.
  RTL is already a live concern here — see `applyLocale` in
  [i18n/index.ts](../../apps/web/src/i18n/index.ts).
