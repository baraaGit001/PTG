## Open questions / follow-ups

- **PV is an integer.** `Product.pointsAwarded` is `Int`, but the app shows
  fractional PV (15.8, 10.5). Seeded values are rounded (16, 11, …). Making
  PV decimal touches order lines and bonus calculation, so it was left alone —
  worth deciding deliberately.
- **Seven of eight products have placeholder artwork.** Only OCUZ had real
  assets. The rest render `placehold.co` tiles until someone supplies images.
- **Prices for OCUZ are inferred**, not sourced: ZAR 756.00 / 1,080.00 with
  21 PV, chosen to sit on the lineup's `PV ≈ price/36` line. Needs confirming
  against the real price list.
- The app's **Fulfillment Orders** screen is much richer than ours (Actual
  Buyer / Payer / Service Center blocks, pickup code, delivery fee, distance
  and risk level, payment method). Ours is a 39-line list.
- Product cards still lack the app's **quick add-to-cart** button and PV line;
  Goods lacks the **Price / Sales / In Stock** sort tabs (i18n keys are
  already in place); Cart lacks **select-all** checkboxes and per-line PV.
- Nest watch mode keeps losing `:3001` to `EADDRINUSE` on restart — the old
  process doesn't release the port fast enough on Windows.
